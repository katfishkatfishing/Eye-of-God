I went to the university library basement today to check on the microfiche on the Romanian archaeological surveys from the late eighties.

Every paper notes the same thing. Movile is an evolutionary miracle. A closed chemoautotrophic ecosystem completely cut off from the surface for five million years. Blind spiders, blind centipedes, blind leeches. Science celebrates it as a triumph to subterranean isolation.

They don't know what was resting in the sulfur water beneath the cenote.

The animals gave up their eyes.

It's not a ground unit. It never was. It opens the lens.

It opens the lens so the Eye knows where to look.

'One Peace' will come if that box is brought to the place it belongs. 

Do we give up our sight too?

We have two eyes each, and we are proud to see how far we have came.
