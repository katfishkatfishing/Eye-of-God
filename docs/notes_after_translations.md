[TRANSCRIBED FROM DR. SKRAIL's NOTES]

Trithemius and his circle weren't trying to communicate with angels. They witnessed the flare, but they only had parchment and candlelight. They looked at the sky through smoked glass, saw the Eye opening, and mistook the horror for the peace from God.

They built boxes to draw the gaze down from the sky.

They thought if the eye looks fully upon the world, the crying will stop. The flesh will stop rotting, the sins will stop breeding, because there will be no more suffering if we had no capacity to desire.

This is what they meant by 'One Peace'. A single, unbroken silence across the globe.
