# ARCHIVE INVENTORY: ITEM-1999-CHEN-MONOGRAPH
**Source:** Found inside Prof. Skrail's steamer trunk, wrapped in an anti-static ESD bad beneath three layers of vulcanized rubber.
**Details:** 120 page thermal bound lab monograph.
**Condition:** Severe degradation. Pages 1-68 contain standard radio astronomy formulas, RF circuit schematics, and high frequency wave guide designs. Pages 69-85 show heavy chemical burns, oily fingerprints containing dried amber grease, and severe thermal warping.

*Note by Khallenghar:* This isn't history coursework. Skrail kept this manual like a liturgical book. I've scanned the table of contents, the preceding technical pages, and page 74, which had the corner folded down until the paper tore.
